# 🎯 Business Problem & Solution

## The Problem

SaaS companies lose 15–25% of revenue annually to preventable churn.
Without systematic scoring, Customer Success Managers (CSMs) work reactively:
- No visibility into which accounts are at risk
- No financial impact quantification per account
- No prioritized action playbook
- Upsell opportunities buried in noise

**Result**: $1.7M/year lost to avoidable churn with no systematic defense.

## The Solution: Revenue Intelligence

**From**: Reactive CSM firefighting  
**To**: Systematic, data-driven account prioritization

```
1. Score every account daily (risk 0–100)
2. Classify risk tier (CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW)
3. Assign recommended action + owner + urgency
4. Calculate expected recovery per action
5. Surface top 20 accounts requiring immediate action
6. Monitor model health + data drift daily
7. Auto-retrain when models degrade
```
